We are a theoretical research group in the field of open quantum systems, at the University of Strathclyde (Glasgow, Scotland). We have a particular focus on developing novel numerical techniques for studying many-body effects Some of the areas we have recently been intrested in are: non-Markovian sytems, tensor network and machine learning approaches for steady states, phase transitions in dissipative systems, open Dicke models and organic polaritons.

We are part of the <a href="http://cnqo.phys.strath.ac.uk/" target="_blank">Computational Nonlinear and Quantum Optics group (CNQO)</a> at the University of Strathclyde.
